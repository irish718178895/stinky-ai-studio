# Updating to v0.8

Copy `server.js`, `package.json`, `README.md`, `UPDATE-v0.8.md`, and the three files under `public/` over v0.7. Do not replace `data/`. Run `npm install`, validate with `node --check`, restart Node.js, and hard-refresh the browser.

The new **Generate missing scene images** control processes scene prompts sequentially through ComfyUI and automatically assigns each saved image to its scene.
