# Stinky AI Studio v0.10 — Background Music Studio

## Added

- Project-local music uploads (MP3, WAV, M4A, AAC, OGG, and FLAC)
- Browser preview and track library
- Track selection per render
- Adjustable music volume
- Automatic looping to the full video length
- Configurable fade-in and fade-out
- Optional narration ducking using FFmpeg sidechain compression
- Music metadata on rendered videos

## Notes

Music files are stored under `data/projects/<project-id>/music/`. Existing projects and videos remain compatible. Use music you own or have permission to use.
